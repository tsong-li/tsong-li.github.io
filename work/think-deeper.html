Think Deeper: keep the answer in view.

Keep high-level process explanations easy to review alongside the answer.

Three layouts, compared in sequence.

Watch each full exploration to compare the reading trade-offs.

Inline

Advantage: The explanation stays in the conversation. Trade-off: As the explanation grows, it pushes the answer down and requires more scrolling.

Side pane

Advantage: A separate pane can hold a longer explanation. Trade-off: The overlay obscures the answer, so readers cannot compare both at once.

Side by side

Team decision: The team adopted this direction. Advantage: The answer and explanation stay visible together. Trade-off: The layout needs enough width, so narrower contexts need another arrangement.

01 / The problem

More context, without losing the answer.

Think Deeper aimed to present high-level process explanations alongside an answer. The challenge was to let people review those explanations without interrupting their task.

I focused on user-facing explanations of how the response was organized, separate from the model's hidden reasoning.

02 / The decision

Three layouts brought the trade-offs into focus.

I used the same task to explore inline explanations, a side-pane overlay, and a side-by-side layout. This made their effects on the reading flow easier to compare.

My principle was to keep the answer in focus and the process explanation available to review.

Inline explanations pushed the answer down as they grew, while the overlay obscured it. The side-by-side layout kept both visible for comparison, but needed enough width. In narrow containers, the two areas needed a different arrangement.

03 / The framework

The principle should travel across screens.

I explored the approach in desktop chat, Pages side chat, and mobile entry points and layouts. The arrangement could change as long as the explanation stayed accessible without interrupting the main task.

On phones, the exploration uses a “See my thinking” entry to open a separate sheet for the process explanation. Scrolling reveals source details and a comparison table without squeezing desktop columns into the phone layout.

04 / The outcome

The team chose the side-by-side direction.

The team adopted my recommendation. During rollout, the work also helped the Think Deeper squad coordinate with related teams.

I learned to keep the answer as the main focus, with the explanation close at hand for anyone who wants to review it.
