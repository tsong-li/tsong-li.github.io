Copilot Daily: make quiet playback clear.

When enabling sound requires a user action, show the mute state and playback progress clearly.

Product
Microsoft Copilot Daily
Contribution
Web playback guidance and transcript progress
Focus
Mute state, sound-on guidance, and scrolling subtitles
Design question
How can people tell that playback is underway when the sound is off?

In this case

01 / When playback seemed inactive 02 / Make sound and progress visible 03 / Results after rollout

01 / When playback seemed inactive

A silent start could feel like no start.

On the web, browser restrictions could let playback begin silently until the user took action to enable sound. Without a transcript or progress feedback, people had little indication that it was playing and could leave thinking nothing had happened.

The player needed to show whether sound was on and make it clear that the briefing was already playing.

02 / Make sound and progress visible

Show that listening has started, even when it is quiet.

I shipped two changes. I repositioned the player buttons to make the mute and unmute controls prominent, with tips explaining the muted state and the action needed to enable sound. I also added scrolling transcript subtitles so people could see that playback had started and follow its progress.

The wider listening experience keeps sources visible on desktop and includes an AI fallibility notice on mobile. Per-chapter transcripts also shipped, allowing people to read each chapter while listening.

The supporting layouts adapt to different screen sizes, themes, and text lengths.

03 / Results after rollout

Playback duration rose as first-chapter bounce fell.

After rollout, playback duration increased by 214%, while first-chapter playback bounce decreased by 49%.

These figures were reported after the combined changes rolled out. No baseline, measurement period, or attribution to individual features was provided.

The results supported making a silent start easier to recognize, with a visible mute state and clear signs that the briefing was progressing.
