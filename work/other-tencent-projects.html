01 / Tencent Maps

Tencent Maps & InteliRide

I was responsible for software experience design for Tencent Maps and InteliRide. Working with the team on two in-car devices, I designed interactions around drivers’ habits and needs, covering vehicle maintenance, fuel use, fault detection, and advanced driver assistance (ADAS).

02 / TAIQ smart figurines

TAIQ smart figurines

For Tencent’s TAIQ smart speaker project, I designed the interaction between the mobile app and the device, with a focus on connectivity and the automatic generation and editing of Honor of Kings highlight videos.

I also took part in the in-car voice collaboration with Neusoft, exhibition planning, advertising shoots, and factory visits, contributing across software, hardware, and product promotion.

03 / HCI course

A year-long HCI course

I organized a 12-month HCI course at Tencent, inviting Dr. Kim Jin-Soo, an HCI specialist and former CEO of Yahoo Korea, to teach the program. My work included coordinating the translation of Korean course materials, supporting curriculum design, running the sessions, and providing online support throughout the year.

04 / Department website & WeChat

MXD website & WeChat channels

I initiated and led MXD’s brand-building work, creating the department’s website, WeChat official account, and video channel from the ground up. I managed the teams responsible for the website and official account across Beijing, Chengdu, and Shenzhen.

I also coordinated the annual showreel, from scripting and footage review to the final edit review, and organized its promotion and publication on WeChat’s video platforms.
