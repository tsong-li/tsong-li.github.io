Approach

From ambiguity to something useful
01 / Judgment

Begin with the decision.

I start with the decision a person needs to make and keep the design focused on it as information, features, and constraints accumulate.

02 / Systems

Design for the system around it.

I turn individual design decisions into reusable rules, accounting for language, context, components, and the people who maintain them. This lets a solution work beyond its original use.

03 / Collaboration

Make the work tangible early.

I use prototypes to help teams compare ideas and agree on a direction before handing the work to engineering.
